# Men's Group Blueprint: Deconstructing Patriarchy & Cultivating Intimacy

This blueprint serves as a foundational guide for establishing an all-male peer group dedicated to dismantling patriarchal conditioning, building emotional intelligence, and fostering deep intimacy with loved ones.

---

## ✊ Part 1: Core Group Foundations & Agreements
Before diving into deep personal sharing, establish a "safe container" by explicitly agreeing to the following communication boundaries:

* **Absolute Confidentiality:** What is shared in the circle stays entirely within the circle. No exceptions.
* **Speaking from the "I":** Focus on personal experiences, feelings, and actions. Use statements like *"I feel..."* or *"In my experience..."* instead of generalized theories (*"Men usually..."*).
* **Listening Without Fixing:** Resist the urge to give unsolicited advice, solve problems, or minimize discomfort. Allow each man to sit with and express his full range of emotions.
* **Emotional Accountability:** Take full ownership of your actions, mistakes, and emotional reactions without deflecting or shifting blame onto others.

---

## 🛠️ Part 2: Practical Vulnerability Exercises
Integrate these structured exercises into your meetings to safely bridge the gap between abstract concepts and lived emotional reality:

### Exercise 1: Coming Out of Hiding
* **Objective:** Identify and share the hidden parts of yourself that patriarchal conditioning has taught you to suppress.
* **Format:** Pair up and sit across from each other, maintaining consistent eye contact.
* **Steps:**
  1. **Partner A** responds to the question: *"What are the ways that I avoid being fully seen?"* Share continuously for 90 seconds while Partner B listens actively and silently.
  2. **Partner A** then responds to the follow-up question: *"What are the parts of myself that I am most afraid to let others see?"* Share for another 90 seconds.
  3. **Switch roles** and repeat the steps.
  4. Spend 5 minutes together processing what it felt like to verbalize these hidden aspects.

### Exercise 2: Mapping Emotional Armor
* **Objective:** Unpack how anger is often used to mask underlying "feminine-coded" emotions like grief, fear, or shame.
* **Format:** Solo reflection followed by a whole-group go-around.
* **Steps:**
  1. Take 5 minutes of silence to journal about a recent conflict or moment of intense frustration.
  2. Identify the primary surface-level emotion (e.g., anger, annoyance, irritation).
  3. Dig deeper to look for the "underground" emotions beneath that armor. Ask yourself: *Was there unexpressed grief? Did I feel invisible? Was I deeply afraid of failing or being rejected?*
  4. Share one discovered "underground" emotion with the larger circle.

## 📚 Part 3: Curated Resource Library

### Foundational Literature
* **[The Will to Change: Men, Masculinity, and Love](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Will_to_Change.html?id=G28LTQltyVAC) by Bell Hooks:** An essential examination of how patriarchal culture deprives men of the capacity for emotional intimacy and true self-knowledge.
* **[How Can I Get Through to You?](https://books.google.com/books/about/How_Can_I_Get_Through_to_You.html?id=mwu8w4NTceYC) by Terrence Real:** Focuses on closing the structural communication and intimacy gap that traditional masculine socialization builds between loved ones.
* **[For the Love of Men](https://www.tippingthescalespsychotherapy.com/free-resources/resources-for-men-deconstructing-the-patriarchy) by Liz Plank:** A sociological, modern blueprint for moving away from toxic traits and shifting toward a mindful masculinity.
* **[Sister Outsider](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Sister_Outsider/kE3ek_-FGWgC?hl=en&gbpv=1&dq=sister+outside&printsec=frontcover) by Audre Lorde:** Essential essays exploring the constructive uses of raw emotion, vulnerability, and non-sexual emotional interconnectedness.

### Secondary Reading List: Lived Experiences & Partner Narratives
* **[Know My Name](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Know_My_Name/XUCzDwAAQBAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&dq=know+my+name&printsec=frontcover) by Chanel Miller:** A profound, raw memoir documenting the human cost of patriarchal structures. Essential for practicing deep emotional attunement and believing a woman's lived reality.
* **[Men Explain Things to Me](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Men_Explain_Things_to_Me/XNRWAwAAQBAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&dq=men+explain+things+to+me&pg=PA8&printsec=frontcover) by Rebecca Solnit:** A sharp look at how men systematically silence, interrupt, or minimize women in dialogue, helping men identify their own conversational defaults.
* **[An Honest Woman: A Memoir of Love and Sex Work](https://www.google.com/books/edition/An_Honest_Woman/YxjdEAAAQBAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&printsec=frontcover) by Charlotte Shane :** Shane shifts seamlessly from her time as an escort to her personal experiences in marriage. She holds up a mirror to how men—even those with the best intentions—rely on everyday dishonesty or a partner’s emotional labor to keep themselves feeling secure.
* **[Hunger: A Memoir of (My) Body](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Hunger/XJJVCgAAQBAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&printsec=frontcover) by Roxane Gay :** A deeply vulnerable memoir detailing the aftermath of childhood trauma, body image, and navigating a society that demands women be small, compliant, and digestible.

### Structural Curricula & Organizing Frameworks
* **[Curriculum for a Feminist Men's Group](https://hivefund.org/resources/resources-for-dismantling-patriarchy) by Matt Goldfield and Jonah S. Boyarin:** A direct syllabus tailored explicitly for men ready to unpack systemic gender privilege.
* **[Against Patriarchy Tools](https://challengingmalesupremacy.org/resources/) by Chris Crass:** A practical toolkit designed to help men actively organize and challenge male supremacy.
* **[The Live Respect Curriculum](https://www.acalltomen.org/) by A Call to Men:** Education and frameworks centered on building healthy emotional transparency and preventing relationship harm.
* **[For the Love of Men: From Toxic to a More Mindful Masculinity]() by Liz Plank:**  Plank details exactly how patriarchal structures cause men to suffer from higher rates of isolation, suicide, and emotional illiteracy. It is an excellent bridge text because it combines a critique of male privilege with an urgent plea for male healing.
* **[Boys Will Be Boys: Power, Patriarchy and Toxic Masculinity](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Boys_Will_Be_Boys/rB-9DwAAQBAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&printsec=frontcover) by Clementine Ford:** An incisive look at how patriarchal expectation harms boys from a young age by shrinking their permissible emotional spectrum.

### Model Organizations & Communities
* **[The ManKind Project](https://mankindproject.org/mens-groups-2/):** Renowned for its Integration Groups (I-Groups) focusing on deep accountability, emotional growth, and healthy brotherhood.
* **[Hey Brother Co.](https://www.heybrotherco.com/virtual-events):** A community space that runs virtual circles exploring male isolation, media-driven masculinity, and patriarchy.

## 🗓️ Part 5: Turn-Key 90-Minute First Meeting Agenda
Use this step-by-step layout to run a seamless, impactful inaugural session:

* **00:00 – 00:15 | Welcome & Foundation (15 mins)**
  * Welcome the men. Read through **Part 1: Core Group Foundations & Agreements** out loud. Have every participant verbally assent to the boundaries.
* **00:15 – 00:30 | Brief Check-In (15 mins)**
  * Go around the circle. Each man shares his name and a 1–2 sentence answer to: *"What emotions or physical sensations am I carrying into this room right now?"*
* **00:30 – 00:50 | The Core Discussion (20 mins)**
  * Introduce the group's mission. Pose this opening prompt for open floor sharing: *"In what ways did your upbringing teach you to hide vulnerability, and how has that impacted your current relationships with loved ones?"*
* **00:50 – 01:15 | Vulnerability Practice (25 mins)**
  * Run **Exercise 1: Coming Out of Hiding** (detailed in Part 2). 
* **01:15 – 01:30 | Checkout & Intentions (15 mins)**
  * Close the meeting by passing a talking piece. Each man shares: *"One insight I am taking away, and one micro-action I will take this week to practice emotional intimacy with a loved one."*
